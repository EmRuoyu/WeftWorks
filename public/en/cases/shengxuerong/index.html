St.Edenweiss Group · Knit sampling

St.Edenweiss Group | From knit swatch to polo rendering

A swatch shows the stitches up close. A garment rendering shows where the pattern falls on the body and sleeves. This sampling example from Ningxia St.Edenweiss International Enterprises Group Co., Ltd. puts the two views side by side for design review.

Reading the sample sheet

Ivory polo: follow the motif

Find the large diamonds and zigzags in the lower-left swatch, then trace them across the chest and sleeves in the rendering. The placket shows where the repeat breaks at the center front.

Heather-gray polo: compare the bands

The lower-right swatch shows several narrow bands. In the rendering above, you can compare their spacing across the chest and see how the lighter stitches read against the gray ground.
