Engineering woven into your work

Weft is the crosswise thread in woven fabric. Starting with apparel workflows, we provide hands-on AI engineering for traditional businesses. You work directly with the developers, beginning with one spreadsheet, one garment, or one store.

Our approach to working together

Forward Deployed Engineering

An FDE — Forward Deployed Engineer — works closely with a customer’s team to understand the work and build solutions in context. We bring that approach to apparel: spend time with your people, build a small working version, and refine it together in daily use.

Understand the work

Follow a real task with your team. Map the inputs, handoffs, and decisions that matter.

Build alongside you

Turn one concrete need into a small tool. Review it with the people who will use it.

Connect and refine

Decide what to integrate, document the workflow, and improve it as the team puts it to work.
