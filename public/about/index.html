About WeftWorks

An AI partner for
factories and exporters.

WeftWorks is an independent AI and digital transformation studio for apparel manufacturers and export companies. We work alongside the people who quote, sample, make and ship each order.

Weft / The crosswise thread

Built with the people behind each order

Weft is the crosswise thread in woven fabric. Our engineers work with merchandisers, sample rooms and factory planners to connect the records they rely on, from the buyer’s first email to the final packing list.

The people doing the work shape the tool.

Follow the order

Sit with sales, merchandising and production as one order changes hands. Together we trace where information is entered twice or a buyer’s update gets missed.

Use the records you have

Start with buyer emails, style sheets and production trackers. Agree on the order and style numbers that connect them before choosing what to automate.

Keep approvals explicit

Let AI draft a revision sheet or flag conflicting quantities. Your designer approves the sample, and your export team signs off on the shipment documents.

A direct conversation

Where does an order slow down?

Bring a buyer inquiry, a sample revision or a production tracker. We’ll trace one handoff with you and identify what a first pilot could cover.

Our approach to working together

Forward Deployed Engineering

A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) works inside a customer’s workflow. For an apparel exporter, that could mean following a buyer’s revision from the merchandiser to the sample room, testing a tool on past orders, and refining it with the people who use it.

Understand the work

Follow a real task with your team. Map the inputs, handoffs, and decisions that matter.

Build alongside you

Turn one concrete need into a small tool. Review it with the people who will use it.

Connect and refine

Decide what to integrate, document the workflow, and improve it as the team puts it to work.
